The mission

The knowledge is global.
The tools
have not been.

An oncologist in Lisbon, São Paulo or Toronto faces the same clinical questions as one in Boston. The evidence is the same. The guidelines increasingly converge. But the regulatory reality — what is approved, what is reimbursed, what trials are accessible — is entirely different.

Most clinical AI tools answer that question from a US frame of reference. SilverCancer is designed to answer it from your frame of reference — wherever you are.

The agent knows your regulator, your reimbursement authority, your guideline bodies and your trial registries. It reasons accordingly, without being asked to.

Principles
I

Country-adaptive by default

The same question gets a different, locally accurate answer depending on where you practice. This is not a filter — it is how the agent thinks.

II

Oncology-specific depth

Built exclusively for oncology and haematology. Not a general medical tool extended to cancer. Every capability is designed around how oncologists actually work.

III

GRADE-graded, always

Every answer states the certainty of evidence and why. Not just what the evidence says — how certain we are. The difference matters for clinical decisions.

IV

Decision support, not replacement

SilverCancer synthesises evidence. The clinical decision belongs to the clinician. This is not a legal disclaimer — it is a position on what AI in medicine should be.
